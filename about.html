About C-DOT Systems

28+ years of trust, innovation, and technology excellence across India.

Our Story

Future-Forward Solutions

Welcome to C-DOT Systems, where our 28-year legacy in IT hardware, networking, and infrastructure has been a beacon of excellence and innovation.

As a trusted partner for IT and non-IT businesses — large and small — we've built our reputation by aligning with leading MNCs, ensuring the highest standards of quality and reliability.

Continuing our promise of 'making IT simple', we offer an accessible approach to cloud technology and advanced solutions — eliminating complexity and making technology approachable for all.

Our Journey

Milestones & Achievements

1996
Founded in Pune
C-DOT Systems established with a vision to make IT simple for Indian businesses. Started with hardware distribution and IT support.
2000s
Networking & Infrastructure
